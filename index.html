How to use the spreadsheet

Check the listing and fit

Open the item on HipoBuyFind and confirm the color, size and current price. Lay a garment that fits you flat and compare its chest width, body length and sleeve length with the seller’s chart. Check whether the chart gives flat widths or full circumferences.

Review QC photos

If your agent provides warehouse quality-control photos, compare them with your selected variant. Check labels, seams and visible defects. If a measurement or detail is unclear, ask about an extra photo and check the return window before approving shipment.

Estimate the delivered cost

Add the item price, domestic delivery, any agent fees, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties. For bulky items such as puffer jackets, ask whether the carrier charges by actual weight or volumetric weight.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference for organizing product listings by category, price and source link. This independent guide displays HipoBuyFind listings and links directly to their source pages.

Can I order one of these listings through KakoBuy?

Open the HipoBuyFind product page and look for the original marketplace URL. If you find it, check whether KakoBuy accepts the URL and can quote the specific variant. Availability through KakoBuy varies by listing.

Why can the USD amount differ from checkout?

The USD figures are approximate conversions of the listed CNY prices. Exchange rates, payment fees, shipping charges and taxes can affect what you pay.
